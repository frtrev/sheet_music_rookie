Sheet Music Rookie

Sing it. See it. Shape it.

Create musical notation from a melody using your device microphone. Review notes and rhythms, shape your score, and hear it played back with local instrument sounds.

Prepared for iPhone, iPad, and Android release. Store download links will be added after publication.

Features

  • Treble, Bass, Alto, and Tenor clefs with octave ranges.
  • Live note detection and phrase review, processed on your device.
  • Pitch, duration, rests, ties, lyrics, and undo/redo controls.
  • Tempo, key signature, time signatures, and metronome.
  • Instrument playback, practice, and melody tests.
  • Local composition saving and sharing; MusicXML and MuseScore import.

Designed for one clear melody at a time. Review detection results before relying on the notation.
