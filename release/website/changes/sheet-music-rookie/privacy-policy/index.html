Privacy Policy

Sheet Music Rookie is published by Francisco Trevino under NeoTheOne. The app creates musical notation from microphone input and provides score editing, playback, and practice tools.

Microphone input

Microphone permission is requested when you start a listening feature, such as recording, practice, or a melody test. Audio samples are processed on your device to estimate musical pitches and note durations. Live analysis frames and phrase-review audio buffers are held in memory; the app does not save microphone recordings as audio files or send them to a server. Saved compositions contain derived notes, timing, pitch/confidence information, lyrics you enter, and score settings. Denying microphone permission leaves score editing and saved-score use available.

Local storage and playback

Compositions are saved as compressed MusicXML files in the app’s documents directory. Detection and practice settings are stored in local preferences. Imported scores are copied to temporary local storage. Playback uses the bundled GeneralUser GS SoundFont to generate audio locally; this synthesized playback audio is distinct from microphone recordings.

Data collection and third parties

This version has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated upload service. Its Flutter plugins provide recording, playback, preferences, file storage, sharing, and screen-awake functions. No third-party analytics or advertising SDK was identified in the inspected dependencies. The app does not collect personal information for the publisher. User-entered titles or lyrics may contain information you choose to include.

Sharing, backups, and external services

Sharing a saved composition opens the operating system’s share interface. If you select another app or service, it receives that file under its own privacy practices. The operating system or device backup settings may back up local documents and preferences. Copies you export, share, or back up are outside this app’s control.

Retention and deletion

Saved compositions and preferences remain until removed from the device. This version does not offer an individual saved-file deletion control in its library. Clear app storage in Android Settings, or use Delete App on iOS to remove local app data; iOS Offload App retains documents. Remove exported files and backup copies separately. There is no online account to delete.

Support communications

If you email support, your email provider transmits your address, message, and attachments to the publisher. Send only information you choose to share. You can request deletion of support messages by contacting ftrevino@yahoo.com; legal or security obligations may require retention.

Children and changes

The app can be used to learn music without an account. It does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. This policy will be updated if the app’s practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, email ftrevino@yahoo.com.
