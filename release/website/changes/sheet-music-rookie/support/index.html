Sheet Music Rookie Support

Turn a melody into sheet music, then edit, play, and practice it offline.

Get started

  1. Tap Create. Choose Treble, Bass, Alto, or Tenor clef, then an octave range.
  2. Set tempo, key, and time signature for your melody. Start recording when ready and allow microphone access.
  3. Sing one clear note at a time in a quiet room. Pause to edit pitches, durations, rests, ties, and lyrics. Detection is an estimate; review the score.
  4. Save and name your composition. Use Open to reopen it or the share button in the saved-score list to export it.
  5. Open compatible MusicXML or MuseScore files from another app with Sheet Music Rookie.

Microphone troubleshooting

On iOS, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, or the app’s Settings entry. On Android, check Settings → Apps → Sheet Music Rookie → Permissions → Microphone. Also check the system microphone privacy switch. Close other recording apps, enable access, then return and tap Retry in the Microphone unavailable card below the score controls. Reopen the score or restart if needed. Editing works without microphone permission.

Frequently asked questions

Does it need internet or an account?

Core notation, local saving, playback, and practice work offline with no account.

Does it record my voice to a file?

No microphone audio file is saved. Notes and timing derived from your performance can be stored in a composition. Phrase analysis temporarily buffers audio in memory.

Why is a note or rhythm wrong?

Use a quiet room, sing one note at a time, choose the correct tempo and range, and adjust detection settings. Polyphonic music and noisy recordings are not reliable inputs. Review and edit the result.

Where are my scores?

In local app storage. Save before leaving and share a copy before clearing data or deleting the app. There is no cloud account recovery.

How do I remove data?

Clear app storage on Android or use Delete App on iOS. Exported and backed-up copies must be removed separately. There is no individual saved-score deletion button in this version.
