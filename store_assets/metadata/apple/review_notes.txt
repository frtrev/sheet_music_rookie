No account, login, server, or subscription is required. Create → choose a clef → choose an octave range opens the score editor. Start a listening feature to trigger microphone permission; sing one clear note at a time in a quiet room. Core editing and local playback remain usable without microphone access. Use Insert Note to create notes without audio input. Save names the composition; Open lists locally saved scores. Compatible MusicXML/MuseScore files can be opened from another app. Detection is approximate and results are editable. No microphone recording is uploaded. Simulator screenshots use a locally saved original sample exercise, with no fabricated listening state.
